# Plan of Action (POA) — guidelines

The POA is a **short document with your week-wise plan** for SoS, including planned report submissions. It doesn't have to be exact or rigidly followed — it can shift as the summer progresses. Any tool works (Word, Google Docs, Markdown), but **LaTeX is highly recommended**.

## Required elements

1. **Checkpoints:** treat **midterm as mid-June** and **endterm as mid-July**, and include both in the POA.
2. **References are mandatory:** list the books / lecture series / online resources you plan to use during SoS.
3. **Be realistic *and* comprehensive.** Certification depends on how much of your POA you actually accomplish, so don't promise the moon — but it also needs to be substantial enough not to get rejected outright.
4. **Get it reviewed by your mentor (me) before submitting.** Send me a draft and I'll give feedback.

## Suggested structure

A clean POA usually has:

- **Title + your details** (name, roll number, SoS topic, mentor name)
- **Motivation / goals** — 1 paragraph on why analog electronics and what you want to walk away with
- **Week-by-week plan** — table of weekly topics with rough deliverables
- **Midterm checkpoint** (mid-June) — what's done by then
- **Endterm checkpoint** (mid-July) — final project + report
- **References** — textbooks, lecture series, datasheets, this repo (if you choose to follow it)

## Using this repo

This repo is *one possible* trajectory. You can:

- Adopt the 8-week structure here verbatim
- Cherry-pick weeks and reorder them
- Substitute different topics (e.g. drop the BJT/MOS week if you want to go even deeper into op-amps)
- Pick a different final project entirely

Whatever you choose, **the POA you submit is yours**. This repo just gives you a baseline so you don't have to draft from scratch.

## Reference list — feel free to copy

- **Sedra & Smith**, *Microelectronic Circuits* — primary reference for diodes, BJT, MOS, op-amp basics.
- **Sergio Franco**, *Design with Operational Amplifiers and Analog Integrated Circuits* — primary reference for op-amps and filters.
- **Prof. M. B. Patil's EE204 (IIT Bombay)** — lecture slides + problem sets. https://www.ee.iitb.ac.in/~sequel/course_material.html
- **Horowitz & Hill**, *The Art of Electronics* — broader intuition.
- **Razavi** YouTube lectures on *Fundamentals of Microelectronics* — free, excellent.
- **LTspice** (Analog Devices) for simulation; AD633 datasheet if doing Lotka-Volterra.
- This guideline repo: https://github.com/Vedika-Doke/SoS-26-Analog-Electronics
